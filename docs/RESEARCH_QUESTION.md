# Research Question

## Primary question

Does global broadcast causally improve coordination and stability across
competing specialist processes?

## Secondary questions

1. Does ignition reduce disagreement between modules?
2. Does broadcast stabilize task-relevant representations?
3. Does raising the ignition threshold impair coordination?
4. Does increased competition produce unstable workspace winners?
5. Does disabling broadcast preserve local competence while degrading global
   coordination?
6. Can later PCC-EBID candidate regimes be identified from observable workspace
   dynamics alone?

## Initial candidate mappings

These are hypotheses only.

### Pressure-like candidate

Persistent competition or threshold bottleneck producing constrained but poorly
coordinated behavior.

### Control-like candidate

Successful task-relevant ignition followed by stable global coordination.

### Chaos-like candidate

Unstable workspace winners, high switching, or fragmented broadcast.

No PCC label is treated as established in Experiment 001.
